# GEOL204 — Course Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, a student should be able to:

- Describe what happens across an Imbrication fabric and the depositional mechanisms underlying each stage.
- Explain how carbonate cement affects porosity loss, and distinguish its effect on strength
  from its effect (or lack of one) on grainsize.
- Compare continuous compaction with pausing at successive imbricated fabrics.
- Explain the geometry and diagnostic role of Hummocky stratification.
