# Norling (2019) — Turbidite Emplacement and Bedform Successions

Summary notes for the GEOL204 supplementary reading list.

## What does this paper actually claim?
Bedform steering responds to local density contrasts at the leading edge, and those contrasts share machinery with the deceleration described for mature turbidite emplacement — Norling argues the steering and depositional systems are the same toolkit acting at two scales.

## Why is it on the GEOL204 reading list?
It is the cited source behind the lecture aside that "the parts of a deposit that decide where the sediment goes and the parts that decide when it stops share more machinery than the textbook column suggests."

Citation: Norling, T. (2019). Turbidite emplacement and bedform successions. *Journal of Sedimentary Geology*, 41(3), 210–229.
