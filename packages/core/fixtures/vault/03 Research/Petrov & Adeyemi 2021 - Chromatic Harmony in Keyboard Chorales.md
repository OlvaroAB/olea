# Petrov & Adeyemi (2021) — Chromatic Harmony in Keyboard Chorales

## What is the central argument?
The authors argue that late keyboard chorales use the withheld arrival the way earlier chorales used the written-out ornament — as the moment the listener is trusted with more information than the notated part carries. That gap is treated as a working definition of [[Deceptive cadence]] adapted for a texture with no figuring.

## Why keep this note for MUSTH104?
It's the closest secondary-source framing to how *Chorale No. 12* and *Minuet and Trio* both use delayed arrival rather than declared modulation.
