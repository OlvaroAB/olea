# GEOL204 Past Paper — 2023

Instructions: Answer ALL questions. Total time: 2 hours.

## Question 1 (10 marks)

Outline the sequence of events building an Imbrication fabric, from first contact to recovery.

## Question 2 (10 marks)

Explain how Hummocky stratification preserves the storm record that makes an Imbrication fabric
readable in a cut face.

## Question 3 (10 marks)

A student cannot explain why a bed truncated below a scour still allows the interval nearer the
basin floor to retain primary layering, and its Imbrication fabric, for some distance
beyond it. Account for this using what you know of Bioturbation and Paraconformity.
