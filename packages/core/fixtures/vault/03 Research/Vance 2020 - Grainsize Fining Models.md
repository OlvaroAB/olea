# Vance (2020) — Computational Models of Grainsize Fining

## What does the model add to the lecture picture?
A settling-equation model of how a suspended load decays with distance unless it reaches [[Bioturbation]] depth first — useful for seeing "fining" as a spatial process, not just a tally.

## Where does it connect to the WEEK 2 lecture?
Directly extends the deposit-division discussion — Vance's model is the equation behind the hand-wave in lecture about grains "dropping out" across the basin floor.

Citation: Vance, R. (2020). Computational models of grainsize fining in basin-floor deposits. *Computational Sedimentology Quarterly*, 12(1), 44–61.
