# Aural notes — Chorale No. 12, Phrase One

Quick notes before the seminar:

- The tenor holds the same pitch three separate times — always across the barline.
- Nobody in Phrase One writes a leap larger than a fourth, not once.
- Compare the stepwise descent here with [[Appoggiatura]] discussion for Phrase Two.

Rough question for seminar: is the stillness in the inner parts a choice the composer is making, or a constraint the chorale texture imposes on them?
