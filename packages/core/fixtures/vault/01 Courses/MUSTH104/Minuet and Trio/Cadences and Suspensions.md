# Minuet and Trio — Cadences and Suspensions

## What does "cadence" mean by the closing phrase, compared to the first?
Early in the Minuet it is a punctuation, a formula the phrase is made to repeat until closed. By the closing phrase the same progression describes the one arrival of the Minuet that is genuinely earned — track how the formula's weight inverts.

## How does the Minuet use repeated harmony as a structural device?
Watch for [[Suspension|the returning held notes]] between the second and fifth phrases — nearly identical harmony delivered in different parts, so the listener does the comparison work the Minuet itself never states outright.

## Is the closing progression a plagal cadence, or something else?
Resist calling it a straightforward [[Plagal cadence]] — it does not exist only to soften the arrival; its own approach gets equal weight, which is unusual for the role it is initially set up to play.

Which cadence closes without the leading step that normally earns the arrival?::Plagal cadence

A ==suspension== is prepared, struck and resolved by step.
