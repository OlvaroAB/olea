# Whirlwind Recap — Callout Notes

> [!note]
> Roundness and sphericity are two different numbers — don't conflate them under exam pressure.

> [!tip] Quick way to remember the divisions
> Erosive, coarse, planar, climbing, silt cap — same order every time.

> [!warning]- Common mistake (click to expand)
> Saying cement "fills the pores like grout" is wrong. It's the bridging at grain contacts, not the bulk fill, that creates the strength — see [[Cementation]].

> - Redraw the fining pattern from memory
> - Explain winnowing out loud without notes
> - Compare [[Bioturbation]] against primary lamination in one sentence
