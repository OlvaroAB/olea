## Banding and foliation

Which metamorphic name goes with coarse banding of light and dark crystals?::Gneiss

Foliation
?
The aligned, banded arrangement crystals take on under one-sided pressure.

- A specimen that parts along its foliation into thin plates is a ==schist==.
